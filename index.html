02 / About Us

Built for the community.

Glory of Conflicts is a Roblox game currently in development that aims to simulate the war in Ukraine as a Roblox gaming experience. We are working to create an immersive and engaging experience inspired by the conflict, while keeping the focus on gameplay, teamwork, and the community. Our team is still actively developing the game and putting our best effort into improving the experience. New features, systems, maps, and improvements are being worked on as development continues. Thank you for supporting us while Glory of Conflicts is in development.
